Native equation clipboard conversion uses the unmodified **mathml2omml 0.5.0**
library by Johannes Wilm, licensed under LGPL-3.0-or-later.

- Source and license: https://github.com/fiduswriter/mathml2omml
- Installed library and license: `node_modules/mathml2omml/`

The dependency is distributed as separate JavaScript files and can be replaced
independently. No Microsoft Office stylesheet is bundled or required at runtime.
